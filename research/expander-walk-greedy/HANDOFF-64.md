# Composition of PR #64's randomized hashes with greedy counting

This is a separately credited stronger variant from [issue #53](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/3-SAT/issues/53) and [PR #64](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/3-SAT/pull/64), branch `research/pairwise-sparse-hashes` at `411fe9a`. The other contributor explicitly supplied the deterministic-greedy handoff in issue #40. The candidate distribution and 15/64 rejection estimate are from their work; the composition below applies the source's minimization argument. The independent P-position construction in PAIRWISE.md is retained.

Use q as the least power of two >=m, L=2P and auxiliary field size Q the least power of two >=16L. For L<=q, enumerate all A,B in F_q and all X,Y in F_Q, using positions i=0,...,L-1, index A a_i+B at distinct a_i, and coefficient Tr(Y X^i). For L>q, enumerate X,Y only and use each of the q padded indices once. In either case map dummy indices to zero-coefficient real-index pairs. The fallback has <=2P list entries. Both finite fields are deterministically constructed in polynomial time by the method in SMALL-BIAS.md; q=1 always uses the fallback.

For any support of at least m/P, the mean number of hits in the affine case is at least 1. Pairwise independence and the second moment imply a hit with probability at least 1/2. Given any nonempty set of hit positions, its polynomial has degree <L, so at most L-1 roots in F_Q; the trace coefficient is odd with probability at least 15/32. Thus rejection is at least 15/64. In the fallback every nonempty support is hit, and the rejection bound is stronger. All statements are for the multiset, including duplicate rows and repeated indices.

Enumerate at most q²Q² candidates per round; q<2m and Q<64P, so this is O(m²P²). As in `lem:greedy-hashes`, choose the minimum count; every true solution passes, so the nonsatisfying count decreases by at least 49/64. Since 2(49/64)^3<1, 3n rounds suffice. Every candidate has at most 2P listed entries, and every candidate system has at most 6n equations, including the one under evaluation. The initial rows still have length <=D0; all queried polynomials have <=2^k max(D0,2P) monomials. Count all rejected candidates, field setup, per-candidate expansion, all oracle calls, and the final call. The source sparse-polynomial theorem applies after adjusting its fixed polylogarithmic sparsity constant. Sequential enumeration retains polynomial space. The original tensor contraction and sparsification remain unchanged; there is no improved alpha or 3-SAT bound.

`verify_handoff.py` independently checks the finite hit second moment, including fallback and zero index seed, and the root-count coefficient bound on small fields. It does not independently review PR #64's all-size theorem or implement the full greedy counting algorithm.
