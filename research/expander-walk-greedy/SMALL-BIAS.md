# Polynomial-size coefficient family

This strengthens [PROOF.md](PROOF.md), using the same walk and graph. Put h=2^r, the least power of two at least 2P, and fix a representation of F_h. For each x,a in F_h, make the P-bit string c_j=Tr(a x^j), j=0,...,P-1, using x^0=1 even when x=0. There are h^2 <= 16P^2 strings as a multiset.

For any nonempty position set J, let f_J(X)=sum_{j in J} X^j. This nonzero polynomial has degree at most P-1 and so at most P-1 roots in F_h. For any x with f_J(x) nonzero, the parity sum_{j in J} c_j = Tr(a f_J(x)) is uniform as a varies. Indeed the trace is a nonzero F_2-linear functional: its polynomial X+X^2+...+X^(2^(r-1)) has degree less than h and is not identically zero on the field. Multiplication by a fixed nonzero element is a bijection. At a root the parity is zero. Consequently a uniform string rejects J with probability at least (1-(P-1)/h)/2 >= 1/4. The argument applies to the position set of hits on any fixed walk, including walks with repeated vertices; it does not assume distinct clause indices.

Construct the field deterministically by enumerating all monic degree-r binary polynomials and trial-dividing by monic polynomials of degrees 1 through floor(r/2); an irreducible polynomial exists for every r. The bounded enumeration, field arithmetic and trace evaluation take poly(h,r) = poly(P) time and polynomial space. This avoids an uncharged field-construction oracle.

The walk hits every qualifying violation support with probability at least 1/9 as shown in PROOF.md. Conditional on each hitting walk, the coefficient family rejects with probability at least 1/4, so a candidate rejects with probability at least 1/36, in particular at least 1/40. Enumerate all starts, walks and field pairs; there are q d^(P-1) h^2 lists. A greedy minimizer reduces D by at least 39/40 per round. After 28n rounds, D <= 2^n(39/40)^(28n) < 1 because 2(39/40)^28 < 1. This uses at most 28n q d^(P-1) h^2 candidate count calls plus the final call and at most 31n+1 equations per candidate; each row has P listed pairs. Graph construction, field construction, row generation, all calls and polynomial space have the same accounting as in PROOF.md. This remains exp(polylog n) under the source parameters and does not improve the selected SAT exponent or alpha.

This is a standard polynomial-evaluation small-bias idea, specialized here with a direct root-count proof and a walk-conditioned parity contract. No claim of priority for the generator is made. The tests independently enumerate every nonempty subset for P<=8 over the constructed fields and check the bias, and directly combine all walks and generated coefficients for the small domain in `verify.py`; these checks do not replace the all-size proof.
