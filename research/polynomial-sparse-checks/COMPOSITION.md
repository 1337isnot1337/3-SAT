# Composition with polynomial greedy candidates

This is a conditional cross-PR composition, not a new independently reviewed SAT theorem. Assume the author-checked construction in PROOF.md and the 2P-width candidate family in [PR #51](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/3-SAT/pull/51), `HANDOFF-64.md`, which credits [PR #64](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/3-SAT/pull/64). Neither PR needs to be merged for this cost implication; the cited arguments and accepted upstream counting theorem are inputs.

For a source sparsification leaf with n declared variables and m<=Bn clauses, construct the initial checks in poly(n,m,B) time and space as in PROOF.md. The source parameter P=20000 B² (floor(log2 m)+1)² is polynomial in B and log(m+1). For the credited candidate family set q to the least power of two >=m, L=2P and Q to the least power of two >=16L. There are at most q²Q² lists per round, including the fallback case. Since q<2m and Q<64P, at most

    3n q² Q² + 1 < 49152 n m² P² + 1

calls to the sparse-polynomial count oracle suffice per leaf, including the final count. The algorithm processes one candidate at a time, retains no list of candidates, and keeps at most 6n equations; the input and storage costs are polynomial in n,m,B. Every candidate polynomial, even a rejected one, has degree <=k and at most 2^k max(D0,2P) monomials. For source B=polylog(n), P and D0 are polylog(n), so a fixed source constant c accommodates all candidates. Satisfying assignments pass every row; the source minimization proof and the 15/64 per-assignment rejection bound establish equality of the final common-zero count and the leaf's exact satisfying count.

Charge the source sparsification tree and every leaf separately. A single source sparse-polynomial oracle call still takes n^{O(1)} 2^{O(n log log n / sqrt(log n))} time and polynomial space; the original sparsification has 2^{O(n/sqrt(log n))} times polynomially many nodes. Thus sequential summation of disjoint leaf counts still has the accepted general-density exponent, with polynomial space. Sparse-check setup and candidate enumeration are now polynomial per leaf, whereas the unchanged tensor-query entry work and contraction, the branching tree, and all other source costs remain charged. The fixed-density path is unchanged. Removing this setup overhead alone does not establish alpha>1/2 or even a smaller leading contraction exponent.

The bound assumes both unreviewed cross-PR arguments and the owner-accepted baseline; no full combined implementation, hosted proof review, or new selected result is claimed. The row count, coefficient-list width, and number of calls are all stated explicitly so that future alternatives can be compared without treating a changed preprocessing term as a complete bound.
